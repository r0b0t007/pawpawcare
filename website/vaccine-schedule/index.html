Puppy & kitten vaccine schedule

The core vaccines most puppies and kittens need, at the ages they usually get them — plus a calculator that turns your pet's birth date into rough due dates you can take to the vet.

This is general information, not veterinary advice. Real schedules vary by region, breed, lifestyle, and local law, and your veterinarian sets the plan that's right for your pet. Use the dates below as a starting point for that conversation — not a replacement for it.

Typical core-vaccine ages

Puppies — core vaccines
VaccineProtects againstUsual age
DHPP #1Distemper, hepatitis (adenovirus), parvovirus, parainfluenza6–8 weeks
DHPP #2Same combination, second dose10–12 weeks
DHPP #3Same combination, third dose14–16 weeks
RabiesRabies (often required by law)12–16 weeks
DHPP boosterFirst adult booster~12 months
Kittens — core vaccines
VaccineProtects againstUsual age
FVRCP #1Feline viral rhinotracheitis, calicivirus, panleukopenia6–8 weeks
FVRCP #2Same combination, second dose10–12 weeks
FVRCP #3Same combination, third dose14–16 weeks
RabiesRabies (often required by law)12–16 weeks
FVRCP boosterFirst adult booster~12 months

Non-core vaccines (e.g. Bordetella, Lyme, leptospirosis, FeLV) depend on where you live and how your pet spends its days — ask your vet whether any apply.
