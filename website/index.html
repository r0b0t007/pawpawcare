Records

Snap a vet record. We file the rest.

Point your camera at a vaccination card, prescription, or invoice. PawPawCare reads it and extracts the vaccines, meds, weights, and costs — then shows you exactly what it found.

Nothing is saved until you approve it. Every extracted item gets a checkbox; you stay the editor-in-chief of your pet's file.

Sharing

Hand off care without handing off your phone.

A read-only link with vaccines, meds, and weight history. Your vet opens it in a browser — no app, no account. It expires when you say so.

Caretaker mode

Family sharing

Co-own pets with your household. Everyone logs walks and meals; nobody double-doses the dog.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is PawPawCare free?

The preview is completely free. When we launch, core tracking stays free; some power features may become part of a paid plan.

When does it launch?

We're onboarding waitlist members in small batches right now, ahead of the app-store launch. Join the waitlist and your invite comes with the next batch.

Is it for iOS or Android?

Both. The preview runs on iOS and Android.

Is my pet's data private?

Yes. Your records are yours: share links are opt-in, time-limited, and revocable, and we never sell your data.

What's in the free preview?

The full app: multi-pet profiles, vaccination and medication tracking, weight trends, the records vault with AI scanning, vet and sitter sharing, and the community feed.
